Here I republish what I post on LinkedIn: short analyses of marketing, brand strategy and outdoor events, written from a dual vantage point — that of a communications professional by trade and a trail runner by practice. My lens

Two threads behind every post

The dual perspective

I'm neither a sports influencer commenting on business from a distance, nor a marketing consultant who's never laced up trail shoes. I design brand strategies daily, and I practice the ground truth sincerely, with no pretense of performance. That dual perspective feeds every analysis published here.

Growth vs. preservation

This is the thread that comes up most often in what I write: how outdoor brands and event organizers handle — or fail to handle — the tension between growing and preserving the territories that sustain them. Not activism: a lens, applied to concrete cases, like the debate around UTMB's growth.

Who's behind this

Communications professional, photographer, trail runner

Weekdays: a desk, communication files, brands to bring to life. Weekends: a pack, some trails, and that same urge to tell a story.

I'm not a professional athlete. I don't run a hundred miles a week. I don't win races.

But I'm fascinated by the stories hiding behind the people who make things happen.

My name is Corentin. I work in communications, I take photos, and I run — not fast, not far, but regularly. One day I realized there was a common thread running through all of it: telling the stories that bring people together.

Entre 2 sentiers was born from that realization. I'm currently between two jobs, looking for new challenges in communications, ideally in the outdoor world — sport, adventure, and brands that know how to build a real community. Rather than wait around, I decided to explore.

This project is a space where I talk about outdoor sport: trail running mainly, but also cycling, hiking, climbing, and above all everything that happens behind the miles: marketing, brand strategy, events, photography, community.

Three pillars, one conviction

Understand. I analyze why a race becomes a global brand, why gear makers fight for your next mile, what a simple race bib reveals about the customer experience, and how far a brand or event can grow without damaging the territory that sustains it.

Create. I show how a race photo is worth more than a souvenir, how to turn a sports event into content that lasts all year, why volunteers are a brand's best ambassadors.

Share. I tell my own story: my job applications, my outings, my lessons learned, with one simple conviction: the mountain doesn't belong only to those who go fast or go far.

My goal isn't to have a hundred thousand followers. My goal is for someone, somewhere in an outdoor brand or a communications agency, to read these lines and think: "this person gets our world." That's why I favor consistency and regularity over virality.
